The model is calibrated to the observed discharge, and all further forecasting or planning will be affected by those. Thus, high measurement accuracy is desirable. The problem is that discharge (Q) measurements require qualified personnel and are therefore limited in scope. Also, measurements of high flows are few because of their low frequency and the danger they pose. Continuous hourly or daily measurements of river flow depth (H) are available. Therefore, continuous discharge is calculated through rating curves representing the QH-relation of a river. Sources of uncertainty include the two measurements and the uncertainty caused by the approximation of the rating curve.
