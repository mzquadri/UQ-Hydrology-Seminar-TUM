4. WHY NSE IS NOT REACHING 0.90
----------------------------------------
   The NSE formula:
   NSE = 1 - sum((Sim - Obs)^2) / sum((Obs - mean(Obs))^2)

   Key insight: SQUARED errors mean large peak mismatches
   have disproportionate impact on NSE.

   From the analysis above:
   - Peak flows are underestimated by avg 53.5 m3/s
   - Low flows are overestimated by 3.4 m3/s

   These systematic errors in peaks and low flows prevent NSE > 0.90.
   This is a model structure limitation, not an optimization failure.
